Our mission at Little Sprouts is to give every child a safe, loving and playful start, where curiosity is encouraged and each child grows at their own pace.

Why choose us

  • A caring, qualified team fully trained in early years education, first aid and child safety.
  • Safe, welcoming spaces: bright, secure rooms and a fenced garden designed just for little ones.
  • Learning through play with stories, music, art and outdoor adventures every day.
  • Care for every stage from 6 weeks to 5 years, in rooms matched to your child's age.
  • Daily updates and photos so you always know how your child's day is going.
  • Open 7:30am – 6:00pm, Monday to Friday, to fit around your working day.
