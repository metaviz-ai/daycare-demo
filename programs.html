Our programs

Caring, age-appropriate programs for children from 6 weeks to 5 years. The schedules below are a guide, and we always adapt them to each child.

Infant

Ages 6 weeks – 12 months

Gentle, one-to-one care in a calm, cosy room. We follow your baby's own routine for feeding and sleep, and fill their awake time with cuddles, songs and sensory play.

A typical day

  • 7:30am Arrival, cuddles and quiet play
  • 9:00am Sensory play and tummy time
  • 10:30am Morning nap (following your baby's routine)
  • 12:00pm Lunch and bottles
  • 1:00pm Buggy walk or garden time
  • 2:30pm Afternoon nap
  • 4:00pm Songs, stories and play until home time

Fees: contact us

Toddler

Ages 1 – 3 years

Toddlers are busy explorers. We give them plenty of room to move, make friends and try things for themselves, with gentle support for first words and potty training.

A typical day

  • 7:30am Arrival and free play
  • 9:00am Snack and circle time
  • 9:30am Messy play, art and building
  • 11:00am Outdoor play in the garden
  • 12:00pm Lunch together
  • 12:45pm Nap and quiet time
  • 3:00pm Music, movement and stories until home time

Fees: contact us

Preschool

Ages 3 – 5 years

Our preschoolers build the skills and confidence they need for big school: early reading and numbers, taking turns, and talking about their feelings.

A typical day

  • 7:30am Arrival, breakfast club and free play
  • 9:00am Circle time: news, weather and today's plan
  • 9:30am Early reading and number games
  • 11:00am Outdoor play and nature exploring
  • 12:00pm Lunch (children help set the table)
  • 1:00pm Quiet time and stories
  • 2:00pm Project time: science, art or cooking
  • 3:30pm Free play until home time

Fees: contact us
