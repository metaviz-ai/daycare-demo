About Little Sprouts

A small, friendly daycare where every child is known, loved and encouraged to grow.

Our story

Little Sprouts started with a simple idea: children thrive when they feel safe, loved and free to explore. What began as one cosy room and a handful of children has grown into a warm community of local families.

We've grown, but we've kept the small, homely feel that parents love. We know every child by name, and we celebrate every new word, first step and finished painting.

Our approach

Young children learn best when they're happy, curious and supported. Everything we do is built on three simple ideas.

🧸

Learning through play

Play is how young children make sense of the world. Hands-on activities help them build language, creativity and problem-solving skills.

🌱

Every child is unique

We follow each child's interests and pace, and keep simple notes on their progress so we can plan activities just for them.

🤝

Partners with parents

You know your child best. We share daily updates and photos, and we're always happy to chat at drop-off or pick-up.
